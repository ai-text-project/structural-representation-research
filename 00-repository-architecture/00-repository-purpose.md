# Purpose

The purpose of the **Structural Representation Research Repository** is to provide a dedicated research space for developing, preserving, and reusing structural representations of heterogeneous research objects.

The repository supports a research lifecycle in which research objects are first externalized as explicit structural representations and may subsequently become reusable inputs for comparative research and cross-case synthesis.

The repository therefore supports the following developmental sequence:

```text
Research Objects
        ↓
Structural Representation
        ↓
Canonical Structural Representation
        ↓
Comparative Research
        ↓
Cross-case Synthesis
        ↓
Research Outputs
```

The repository is not intended merely as a storage location for Structural Representation files.

It provides an architectural research space in which Structural Representations can develop into stable research assets and subsequently participate in broader comparative and synthetic research.

---

# Research Object

The primary research objects managed by this repository are **Structural Representations of identifiable research objects or theoretical systems**.

Current examples include:

* AI Textbook
* SOAR
* ACIM
* World Model

Each research object may be represented through a structured sequence of research assets, including where appropriate:

* Structural Skeleton
* Layer Map
* Module Map
* Dependency Map
* Architecture Matrix
* Concept Network
* Structural Topology
* Canonical Structural Representation
* Structural Summary
* Design History
* Validation Assets
* Reference and Provenance Materials

The exact internal structure may vary when required by the characteristics of the represented object, while the repository-level architectural responsibility remains stable.

---

# Canonical Structural Representations

A major responsibility of this repository is the preservation of **Canonical Structural Representations** as reusable research assets.

A Canonical Structural Representation represents the stabilized structural outcome of a Structural Representation process for a particular research object.

Canonical Structural Representations remain associated with their corresponding Structural Representation directories.

They are not duplicated into a separate canonical asset repository layer.

This preserves the principle:

> **One Canonical Asset — One Canonical Location**

Canonical assets may subsequently be referenced by Comparative Research without transferring ownership of the original representation.

---

# Comparative Research

The repository also provides a research space for Comparative Research based on completed or sufficiently stable Structural Representations.

Comparative Research may include activities such as:

* Structural Correspondence
* Functional Correspondence
* Hierarchical Projection
* Comparative Observation
* Difference Observation
* Comparative Summary

Structural Comparison and Comparative Observation may remain conceptually distinguishable while being organized within a common Case-centered Comparative Research layer.

This preserves continuity between comparison, observation, projection, and interpretation without creating unnecessary repository fragmentation.

---

# Cross-Case Development

Individual Comparative Research cases are not assumed to be the final stage of research development.

As comparative cases accumulate, recurring structures, differences, relationships, or higher-order patterns may become observable across multiple cases.

The repository therefore provides architectural space for:

**Cross-case Synthesis**

This layer allows findings from individual cases to develop into higher-order research assets without requiring changes to the fundamental repository architecture.

---

# Position within the Research Program

The Structural Representation Research Repository is an independent research repository within the broader Research Program.

It is architecturally related to, but distinct from, the Hub Repository.

The relationship can be summarized as:

```text
Research Program
        │
        ├── Hub Repository
        │      │
        │      ├── Research Architecture
        │      ├── Methodologies
        │      ├── Governance
        │      └── Program-level Coordination
        │
        └── Structural Representation Research
               │
               ├── Structural Representations
               ├── Comparative Research
               ├── Cross-case Synthesis
               └── Research Outputs
```

The repositories therefore have different responsibilities while remaining part of the same Research Program.

---

# Relationship with Structural Representation Methodology

The **Structural Representation Methodology** remains outside this repository and is maintained within the Hub Repository.

This separation is intentional.

The distinction is:

```text
Structural Representation Methodology
            │
            │ informs
            ▼
Structural Representation Research
            │
            ▼
Structural Representations
            │
            ▼
Canonical Research Assets
```

The Methodology describes how Structural Representation may be performed.

This repository manages the research activities, cases, and assets that emerge through the application and continued development of Structural Representation research.

The repository therefore does not own or replace the Structural Representation Methodology.

---

# Repository Independence

The establishment of this repository is based on **architectural independence**, not on the number of files or folders accumulated in the Hub Repository.

During the development of the Research Program, Structural Representation activities produced increasingly reusable research assets.

Canonical Structural Representations began to acquire research lifecycles independent from the methodology through which they were produced.

These representations subsequently became inputs for Comparative Research.

This produced an identifiable research domain with its own:

* research objects;
* research assets;
* research lifecycle;
* comparative cases;
* downstream synthesis potential;
* and long-term scalability requirements.

Repository separation therefore reflects the emergence of an independent research responsibility.

---

# Repository Responsibilities

This repository is responsible for:

* preserving Structural Representation research assets;
* maintaining identifiable Structural Representation cases;
* preserving Canonical Structural Representations;
* maintaining relevant reference, provenance, and validation materials;
* supporting Comparative Research using Structural Representations;
* preserving Case-level research traceability;
* supporting future Cross-case Synthesis;
* supporting research outputs derived from this research domain;
* and maintaining the architectural integrity of this research space.

---

# Non-Purpose

This repository is **not** intended to:

* redefine the overall Research Program Architecture;
* replace the Hub Repository;
* own the Structural Representation Methodology;
* serve as a general repository for all research assets;
* store unrelated operational resources;
* function as a generic publication repository;
* duplicate Canonical Structural Representations;
* or reorganize research assets solely for operational convenience.

Repository boundaries should follow research responsibility rather than file volume.

---

# Architectural Principle

The repository follows the principle:

> **Repository follows Research Domain.**

A repository should become independent when its research object, responsibility, lifecycle, and long-term development become architecturally distinguishable from the repository in which the activity originally emerged.

The Structural Representation Research Repository represents such a separation.

---

# Long-Term Purpose

The long-term purpose of this repository is to provide a stable and extensible research environment in which additional research objects can be structurally represented without requiring redesign of the repository itself.

Future research objects may be added to the Structural Representation layer, while new Comparative Research cases may reuse existing Canonical Structural Representations.

As the research space develops, Cross-case Synthesis may allow patterns that are not visible within individual representations or comparisons to become observable.

The repository is therefore designed not only to preserve completed research assets, but also to support the continued development of Structural Representation as a reusable research domain.
