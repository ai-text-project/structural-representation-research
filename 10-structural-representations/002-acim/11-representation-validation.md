Instead, this document confirms that the completed Structural Representation has successfully satisfied the established validation methodology and is therefore ready for future Comparative Observation.

# Methodological Observation

The completion of this repository demonstrates that Structural Representation and Structural Representation Validation function as complementary methodological processes.

Structural Representation externalizes organizational architecture.

Structural Representation Validation evaluates the resulting representation through independent external and internal validation.

Together,

these processes establish a reusable methodological foundation applicable beyond the present ACIM case.

---

# Completion Criterion

Representation Validation is considered complete when:

- the Structural Representation has been fully constructed;
- the Reference Layer has completed both External and Internal Validation;
- no unresolved structural inconsistency requiring architectural revision remains.

The completed repository is therefore regarded as a validated Structural Representation suitable for future Comparative Observation.
