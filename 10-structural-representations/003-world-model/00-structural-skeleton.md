# Structural Skeleton

## Project

World Model Structural Representation

---

# Purpose

This document defines the minimum structural skeleton that can be observed from the frozen Representative Resource Set.

The objective is not to explain every interpretation of World Models.

Instead, this document establishes the smallest recurring structural framework that appears consistently across the representative evidence.

This structural skeleton serves as the foundation for all subsequent representation work.

---

# Observation Scope

This Structural Skeleton is constructed exclusively from the frozen Representative Resource Set.

Primary Resource

- Ding et al.
  *Understanding World or Predicting Future? A Comprehensive Survey of World Models*

Complementary Resource

- Demis Hassabis
  Cambridge Lecture Transcript

No additional resources are incorporated at this stage.

---

# Representation Principle

This document records only structures that are repeatedly observable.

It does not attempt to construct a complete World Model architecture.

Likewise, it does not assume that all World Models share one universal architecture.

The representation therefore remains intentionally minimal.

---

# Current Structural Observation

Current observation suggests that World Models consistently involve a recurring interaction among several fundamental structural elements.

Observed recurring elements include:

- World
- Representation
- Understanding
- Prediction

These elements appear repeatedly across the representative resources.

However, their precise structural relationships remain under observation.

---

# Boundary of Observation

At the current stage, the following structural relationships remain intentionally unresolved.

- Search
- Planning
- Decision-Making
- Action

Current evidence does not yet justify determining whether these elements belong:

- inside the World Model;
- outside the World Model;
- or within a larger cognitive architecture.

These questions are deferred to subsequent structural observations.

---

# Structural Boundary

This Structural Skeleton defines only the existence of recurring structural elements.

It does not yet define:

- layers;
- modules;
- dependencies;
- topology;
- canonical architecture.

Those representations belong to subsequent methodological stages.
