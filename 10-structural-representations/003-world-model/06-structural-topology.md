# Structural Topology

## Project

World Model Structural Representation

---

# Purpose

This document records the overall structural topology observed within the frozen Representative Resource Set.

Unlike the Dependency Map, which records prerequisite relationships, and the Concept Network, which records semantic connectivity, the Structural Topology describes the overall structural organization formed by the observed components.

The objective is not to define a finalized World Model architecture.

Instead, it provides a topology-level representation of the current structural observations.

---

# Topology Observation Principle

Structural topology represents stable organizational relationships among observed structural components.

Only recurring structures supported by the representative resources are included.

Unresolved structures remain explicitly identified as pending observation.
