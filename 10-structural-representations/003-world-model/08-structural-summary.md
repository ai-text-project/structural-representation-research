# Purpose

This document summarizes the structural observations obtained through the Structural Representation process.

Unlike the previous representation documents, which record individual structural observations, this document provides a concise overview of the current structural understanding.

The objective is to summarize what has been structurally established, what remains unresolved, and how the current representation should be interpreted.

- Search
- Planning
- Decision-Making

These functions remain intentionally unresolved.

Further structural observation will determine whether they belong:

- inside the World Model;
- outside the World Model;

# Interpretation

The present representation should not be interpreted as a finalized implementation architecture.

Instead, it represents the minimum recurring structural organization that can currently be justified through observation.

The representation therefore remains evidence-driven and intentionally conservative.
