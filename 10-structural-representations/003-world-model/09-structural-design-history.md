# Purpose

This document records the methodological development of the World Model Structural Representation process.

Unlike the structural representation documents themselves, which record observations about the World Model, this document records how the representation methodology was applied and refined during the current mission.

The objective is to preserve methodological decisions for future reuse and validation.

---

# Initial Conditions

The Structural Representation mission began after completion of the Representative Resource Curation process.

The following conditions were established before representation began.

- Representative Resource Set frozen
- Resource Readiness Gate completed
- Role Boundary confirmed
- Structural Representation sequence accepted without modification

This ensured that representation began from a stable methodological foundation.

# Major Methodological Decisions

The following methodological decisions significantly influenced the representation.

## Observation Before Interpretation

Structural observations were completed before architectural interpretation.

No canonical architecture was assumed in advance.

---

## Representation Before Comparison

Structural Representation remained completely independent from Comparative Observation.

No comparison with SOAR, ACT-R, Transformer, Knowledge Graph, or other architectures influenced the representation process.

---

## Canonicalization as an Independent Stage

Canonical Structural Representation was treated as a separate methodological stage.

Rather than emerging implicitly from Structural Topology, canonicalization became an explicit step following completion of structural observation.

---

## Explicit Preservation of Uncertainty

Search, Planning, Decision-Making, and Action remained intentionally unresolved.

Uncertainty was documented rather than eliminated.

---

# Methodological Outcome

The current mission demonstrates that the Structural Representation methodology developed through previous case studies can be applied to the World Model domain without structural modification.

The methodology therefore appears reusable across multiple representation targets.

Further validation through additional domains remains desirable.

---

# Future Methodological Considerations

Future Structural Representation missions may evaluate whether additional stages become necessary.

Possible future investigations include:

- Representation Validation
- Structural Kernel Extraction
- Cross-Domain Canonicalization

These topics remain outside the scope of the current mission.
