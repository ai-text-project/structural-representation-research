# Mission Purpose

This mission prepares representative observational inputs for the Structural Representation of the World Model conceptual domain.

The purpose is not to construct a Structural Representation of World Models.

The purpose is to identify, select, document, and prepare a minimal set of representative resources that can serve as a traceable observational basis for subsequent Structural Representation.

This mission therefore operates immediately before the Structural Representation stage.

# Target Domain

The target domain is:

**World Model**

For the purpose of this mission, World Model is treated as a broad AI research and conceptual domain concerned with representations or models of the world that may support functions such as:

- world understanding;
- internal representation;
- prediction;
- simulation;
- interaction;
- decision-making;
- planning;
- action.

These categories indicate the expected conceptual coverage of the selected resources.

They do not constitute a Structural Representation.

No structural hierarchy or dependency among these concepts is asserted at this stage.

---

# Mission Objective

The operational objective is to establish a concise, publicly traceable, and methodologically defensible observational basis for subsequent World Model Structural Representation.

The mission therefore aims to:

1. identify representative resources for the World Model domain;
2. select a minimal resource set with sufficient conceptual and architectural coverage;
3. preserve both academic-field and broader AGI-oriented perspectives;
4. document why the resources were selected;
5. assess whether the selected resources are ready for Structural Representation;
6. preserve the provenance of the observational inputs; and
7. transfer the completed resource package to the Structural Representation Methodologist.

# Complementarity Principle

The Primary Resource and Complementary Resource should remain distinguishable throughout the subsequent workflow.

The Primary Resource primarily answers:

> How is the World Model research domain organized and understood across the academic field?

The Complementary Resource primarily helps illuminate:

> How can World Models be situated within a broader AI and general-intelligence trajectory involving search, planning, interaction, and action?

These are related but not identical observational perspectives.

The downstream Methodologist should not silently collapse them into a single source perspective.

# Why the Resource Set Is Minimal

World Model research contains a large and rapidly developing literature.

Potential resources include:

- foundational World Model papers;
- model-based reinforcement learning research;
- video-generation models;
- robotics World Models;
- autonomous-driving World Models;
- embodied-agent research;
- cognitive-science perspectives;
- predictive models;
- generative environment models;
- implementation-specific architectures.

The present mission does not attempt to include all such materials.

Instead, it applies:

```text
Representative Coverage
        +
Complementary Perspective
        +
Public Traceability
        +
Manageable Input Size
        >
Exhaustive Literature Collection
```

This protects Structural Representation from unnecessary source expansion.

# Historical / Foundational Resource Boundary

Historically important World Model resources may remain relevant as supplementary or contextual evidence.

For example, foundational World Model research may later help clarify:

- historical development;
- terminology;
- specific architectural mechanisms;
- implementation traditions.

However, historical importance alone does not require inclusion in the frozen Representative Resource Set.

The present mission prioritizes broad conceptual representativeness and structural observability.

# Structural Representation Boundary

This mission does NOT determine:

- the structural skeleton of World Models;
- architectural layers;
- canonical modules;
- dependency relationships;
- concept-network structure;
- structural topology;
- the relationship between representation and prediction;
- the relationship between search and planning;
- the relationship between planning and action;
- whether a single canonical World Model architecture exists.

These questions belong to the downstream Structural Representation process.

This distinction is especially important because World Model is a broad research domain rather than a single fixed architecture.

The downstream Methodologist must determine what level of canonical structural abstraction is justified by the evidence.
