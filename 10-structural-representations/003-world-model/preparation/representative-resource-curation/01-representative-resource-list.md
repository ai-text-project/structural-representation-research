# Target Domain

The target domain is:

**World Model**

World Model is treated here as a broad AI research and conceptual domain rather than as a single fixed architecture.

The selected resources are therefore intended to provide complementary perspectives on:

1. the broader academic organization of World Model research; and
2. the conceptual position of World Models within a broader trajectory toward general artificial intelligence.

The objective is not to establish an exhaustive World Model bibliography.

The objective is to establish a minimal and defensible observational input set for subsequent Structural Representation.

# Resource Role

This resource serves as the Complementary Representative Resource.

Its purpose is not to provide an exhaustive survey of World Model research.

Instead, it provides a broader conceptual perspective on how World Models may relate to the development of increasingly general artificial intelligence.

The lecture discusses World Models in relation to:

- world understanding;
- learning;
- prediction;
- simulation;
- search;
- planning;
- interaction;
- action;
- general intelligence.

This makes the resource particularly useful for observing how World Models are situated within a broader AI-system trajectory.

---

# Transcript Evidence Context

The transcript provides several important observations relevant to the selection of this resource.

Hassabis describes the development of AI systems toward a more general understanding of the world and explicitly refers to these systems as **world models**.

He discusses video-generation systems as learning aspects of the physical world through observation.

He further describes Genie 2 as a World Model concerned with the real world, interaction, and physics.

In the broader AGI discussion, he describes the future combination of general models with search and planning systems capable of operating toward goals in the real world.

The lecture therefore provides a conceptual bridge between:

```text
World Understanding
        ↓
World Model
        ↓
Search / Planning
        ↓
Interaction / Action
        ↓
General Intelligence
```

This bridge is the principal reason for retaining the lecture as a Complementary Representative Resource.

# Why Two Resources Are Retained

The two-resource configuration is retained because neither resource completely substitutes for the other.

Using only the Survey would provide strong academic coverage but would reduce visibility into the broader general-intelligence framing.

Using only the Hassabis lecture would provide a strong conceptual trajectory but would not provide sufficiently broad coverage of the World Model research field.

Therefore:

```text
Academic Breadth
        +
Conceptual / AGI Context
        ↓
Stronger Observational Input
```

The objective is complementarity, not source quantity.
