# Purpose

This document defines the highest-level organizational architecture of the Verbalizable Representations framework.

Unlike subsequent structural assets, this document does not describe individual concepts, experimental procedures, or implementation details.

Instead, it establishes the primary organizational framework that will guide the construction of the Layer Map, Module Map, Dependency Map, Architecture Matrix, Concept Network, Structural Topology, and Canonical Structural Representation.

This Skeleton is derived from the structural observations accumulated throughout Observation Units 01–05.
