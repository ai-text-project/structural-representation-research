# Purpose

This document defines the primary organizational layers of the Verbalizable Representations framework.

Unlike the Structural Skeleton, which establishes the highest architectural framework, the Layer Map identifies the major organizational layers that collectively compose the structural architecture extracted from the paper.

The objective is not to describe experimental results.

The objective is not to define individual implementation details.

Instead, the Layer Map establishes the stable organizational layers that will later contain Functional Modules, Dependency Structures, Concept Networks, and Structural Topology.

# Boundary

This document does not describe:

- implementation details;
- experimental procedures;
- dependency relationships;
- conceptual pathways;
- comparative observations.

---

# Completion Criterion

The Layer Map is considered complete when the highest organizational layers of the paper can be understood independently of experimental details and before any Functional Modules are introduced.

The resulting Layer Map should function as the stable organizational framework upon which all subsequent structural assets are progressively constructed.

---

# Design Status

This Layer Map is generated after Observation Units 01–09.

The organizational layers are considered provisionally stable.

Future refinement is expected to occur within individual layers rather than by introducing additional architectural layers.

---

# Layer Evolution Policy

The organizational layers defined in this document are intentionally independent of specific implementation techniques.

As new structural observations become available, additional Functional Modules may be assigned to existing layers.

However, architectural stability takes precedence over conceptual expansion.

Accordingly,

future refinement should preserve the established organizational hierarchy while improving internal structural precision.
