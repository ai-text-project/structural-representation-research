# Repository Roadmap

**Repository:** Verbalizable Representations Structural Representation Repository

**Version:** v1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the future development roadmap of the Verbalizable Representations Structural Representation Repository.

Unlike the Structural Representation itself, which preserves the completed organizational architecture, this document describes how the repository is expected to evolve as Structural Representation Research progresses.

The objective is not to redefine the completed Representation.

Instead,

this document identifies future directions for repository refinement, validation, and comparative utilization.

---

# Current Repository Status

The repository currently includes:

- Preparation Package
- Structural Representation
- Structural Design History

The completed Structural Representation consists of:

- Repository Architecture Specification
- Structural Skeleton
- Layer Map
- Module Map
- Dependency Map
- Architecture Matrix
- Concept Network
- Structural Topology
- Canonical Structural Representation
- Structural Summary
- Structural Design History

The repository therefore provides a reusable Canonical Structural Representation of the paper.

---

# Immediate Development

The next repository activities focus on structural verification.

Primary tasks include:

- Representation Validation
- Terminology Validation
- Structural Validation
- Agreement Mapping
- Difference Mapping
- Validation Report

These activities evaluate the stability of the completed Structural Representation.

---

# Comparative Observation

After successful validation,

the completed Canonical Structural Representation becomes available for Comparative Observation.

Representative comparison targets may include:

- SOAR
- ACT-R
- World Models
- Predictive Processing
- Active Inference
- Knowledge Graph Architectures
- Other Verbalizable Representation frameworks

Comparative Observation should compare Canonical Structural Representations rather than original research resources.

---

# Methodological Development

The repository also contributes to the ongoing refinement of Structural Representation Methodology.

Future methodological activities may include:

- refinement of Observation Methodology;
- refinement of Representation Validation Methodology;
- comparative analysis across multiple repositories;
- methodological standardization.

---

# Repository Integration

The completed repository functions as one component within the broader Structural Representation Research Program.

Accordingly,

future integration may include:

- Cross-Repository Comparative Observation
- Canonical Representation Library
- Structural Pattern Analysis
- Methodological Case Collections

---

# Long-term Vision

As additional repositories are completed,

a reusable Structural Representation Library may emerge.

Such a library would enable:

- architecture-level comparison;
- reusable organizational analysis;
- methodology validation;
- cross-domain structural research.

The Verbalizable Representations repository therefore serves both as an independent structural resource and as a reusable component of a larger comparative research ecosystem.

---

# Roadmap Principle

Future development should preserve the completed Structural Representation.

New research should extend the repository through validation, comparison, and methodological refinement rather than by modifying the established organizational architecture.

Accordingly,

Observe

↓

Represent

↓

Validate

↓

Compare

↓

Generalize

is considered the long-term developmental pathway of this repository.

---

# Completion Criterion

The Repository Roadmap is considered complete when future development priorities have been clearly identified without altering the completed Structural Representation.

The roadmap should guide future research while preserving the architectural integrity of the repository.
