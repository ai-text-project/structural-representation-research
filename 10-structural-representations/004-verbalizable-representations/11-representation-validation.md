# Purpose

This document validates the completed Structural Representation generated throughout the Verbalizable Representations repository.

Unlike external validation, which compares a Structural Representation against an external reference, Representation Validation evaluates whether the completed Representation faithfully preserves the organizational architecture that progressively emerged throughout the observation process.

The objective is not to reinterpret the original paper.

The objective is not to evaluate experimental correctness.

Instead,

this document evaluates the internal validity of the completed Structural Representation.

---

Representation Validation evaluates the complete Structural Representation process.

- Observation Process
