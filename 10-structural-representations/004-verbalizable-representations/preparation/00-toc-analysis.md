# Purpose

This document analyzes the Table of Contents of the target paper before Structural Representation begins.

Unlike subsequent Structural Representation assets, this document does not analyze the contents of the paper itself.

Instead, it establishes an initial structural hypothesis based solely on the organizational architecture visible in the Table of Contents.

The purpose of this document is to guide the subsequent Structural Observation process while avoiding premature architectural assumptions.

# Observation Principle

At this stage,

only the organizational structure of the paper is observed.

No interpretation of the paper's technical claims is performed.

No Functional Modules are defined.

No Dependency Relationships are inferred beyond the visible organizational structure.

The objective is to establish an initial architectural hypothesis that will later be verified through sequential Structural Observation.

# Expected Representation Strategy

The Structural Representation process will initially focus on identifying:

- the primary architectural entity;
- the organizational responsibilities of each major section;
- structural dependencies among architectural components;
- the developmental progression of the overall architecture.

Detailed Functional Modules will be introduced only after sufficient evidence has been accumulated.

---

# Expected Observation Risks

Several methodological risks are identified before observation begins.

- Overinterpreting chapter titles before reading the paper.
- Confusing methodological procedures with structural architecture.
- Treating repeated terminology as architectural importance without supporting evidence.
- Defining organizational layers prematurely.

These risks will be monitored throughout the Structural Observation process.
