# Purpose

This document defines the observation scope for the Structural Representation process.

Unlike the subsequent Structural Representation assets, this document does not interpret the paper.

Instead, it specifies what organizational elements will be observed throughout each Observation Unit.

The objective is to ensure that structural observation remains systematic, consistent, and independent of premature interpretation.

# Observation Principle

Observation is performed at the level of organizational architecture rather than technical detail.

Accordingly,

the Structural Representation process will observe:

- organizational responsibilities;
- architectural components;
- structural dependencies;
- developmental progression;
- relationships among architectural entities.

Detailed implementation and experimental evidence are recorded only when they contribute to structural organization.

# Primary Observation Targets

Throughout all Observation Units, the following structural elements will be continuously observed.

## Organizational Architecture

The highest-level organizational structure of the paper.

---

## Central Architectural Entity

The primary architectural component around which the paper is organized.

---

## Functional Modules

Organizational components responsible for specific structural functions.

---

## Structural Dependencies

Relationships that establish architectural necessity.

---

## Developmental Progression

The sequence through which the organizational architecture develops across the paper.

---

## Architectural Boundaries

The limits of responsibility assigned to each organizational component.

---

# Observation Policy

During observation,

conceptual interpretation should be postponed whenever possible.

Priority is given to identifying:

- organizational roles;
- structural responsibilities;
- architectural relationships.

Terminological interpretation will follow only after sufficient structural evidence has been accumulated.

---

# Expected Outputs

The completed observation process is expected to provide sufficient evidence for constructing:

- Structural Skeleton
- Layer Map
- Module Map
- Dependency Map
- Architecture Matrix
- Concept Network
- Structural Topology
- Canonical Structural Representation

The observation process should not assume these structures in advance.

They must emerge progressively from the accumulated structural evidence.

---

# Completion Criterion

This document is considered complete when every Observation Unit possesses a clearly defined structural observation target.

The resulting observation scope should guide the entire Structural Representation process while remaining independent of the paper's detailed technical content.

---

# Design Policy

Observation precedes representation.

Representation precedes validation.

Accordingly,

**Observe Structure Before Representing Structure**

is considered the governing principle of this document.
