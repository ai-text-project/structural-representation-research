# Purpose

This document defines the strategy through which structural observations will be transformed into Structural Representation assets.

Unlike the Observation Scope, which specifies what will be observed, this document specifies how the accumulated observations will progressively become a complete Structural Representation.

The objective is not to interpret the paper.

Instead, the objective is to establish a stable methodological process that converts structural observations into reusable architectural representations.

# Strategy Principle

Structural Representation will be constructed progressively.

Architectural components will not be predefined.

Instead,

each representation asset will emerge from accumulated structural evidence obtained during observation.

Accordingly,

representation follows observation.

Architecture follows evidence.

---


# Observation-to-Representation Strategy

Structural observations will be accumulated before architectural decisions are finalized.

The following strategy will be adopted.

## Phase 1

Structural Observation

Identify recurring organizational patterns.

Avoid premature architectural conclusions.

---

## Phase 2

Structural Extraction

Identify candidate layers,

candidate functional modules,

and candidate architectural responsibilities.

---

## Phase 3

Structural Organization

Construct dependency relationships,

architectural responsibilities,

and conceptual organization.

---

## Phase 4

Structural Integration

Integrate all organizational components into a unified Structural Topology.

---

## Phase 5

Canonical Representation

Project the completed organizational architecture into a reusable structural interface.

---

## Phase 6

Representation Validation

Evaluate the completed representation through the established validation methodology.

---

# Representation Policy

Representation assets should emerge only after sufficient structural evidence has accumulated.

Accordingly,

the following principles will be maintained.

- Evidence before abstraction.
- Observation before organization.
- Organization before projection.
- Projection before validation.

---

# Decision Policy

Whenever multiple structural interpretations appear possible,

priority will be given to:

1. organizational consistency;
2. architectural responsibility;
3. structural continuity;
4. repository-wide coherence.

Conceptual interpretation will not override structural evidence.
