# Structural Representations

**Domain:** `10-structural-representations/`  
**Repository:** `structural-representation-research`  
**Status:** Active Research Domain  
**Primary Responsibility:** Construction, preservation, and validation of individual Structural Representations

---

# Purpose

This domain contains Structural Representations of individual research objects.

Each representation case attempts to make the internal organization of a research object structurally observable before that object is used in comparative research.

The central principle is:

> **Represent the structure before comparing the object.**

Structural Representation is therefore treated here as an independent research activity rather than as a preliminary description attached to comparison.

---

# Position within the Repository

The current repository is organized around the following broad research progression:

```text
Representative Resources
        ↓
Structural Representation
        ↓
Representation Validation
        ↓
Comparative Research
        ↓
Cross-Case Synthesis
        ↓
Research Outputs
```

`10-structural-representations/` is responsible primarily for the Structural Representation stage.

Its outputs may subsequently become research interfaces for:

- `20-comparative-research/`
- `30-cross-case-synthesis/`
- `40-research-outputs/`

The structure of an individual representation should not be modified merely to satisfy a later comparative objective.

# Structural Representation Process

Across the more mature cases, a recurring representation pattern is becoming observable.

A provisional generalized form is:

```text
Research Object
      ↓
Evidence / Representative Resources
      ↓
Structural Skeleton
      ↓
Layer Differentiation
      ↓
Module Differentiation
      ↓
Dependency Analysis
      ↓
Architecture Matrix
      ↓
Concept Network
      ↓
Structural Topology
      ↓
Canonical Structural Representation
      ↓
Structural Summary
      ↓
Validation
```

This sequence should currently be understood as an observed representation pattern.

It should **not** be treated as a mandatory template imposed on every research object.

Different objects may require:

- different intermediate representations,
- different evidence structures,
- different validation procedures,
- or different degrees of structural decomposition.

# Representation Assets

Structural Representation may produce several distinct forms of research asset.

## Structural Skeleton

Provides the first high-level structural decomposition of the research object.

Its purpose is orientation rather than exhaustive description.

---

## Layer Map

Differentiates major structural layers where layered organization is supported by the research object.

Layers should be observed rather than imposed.

---

## Module Map

Identifies functional or structural modules where modular differentiation is supported.

A module is not necessarily equivalent to a repository folder, chapter, terminology category, or source-document section.

---

## Dependency Map

Externalizes relationships of structural or functional dependency.

Dependency should be distinguished from:

- hierarchy,
- sequence,
- conceptual similarity,
- and simple co-occurrence.

---

## Architecture Matrix

Provides a structured interface for examining relationships among layers, modules, functions, or other structural units.

The matrix may make relationships visible that are difficult to observe in linear documentation.

---

## Concept Network

Externalizes concept-level relationships where concept interaction is structurally relevant.

A Concept Network should not be interpreted as equivalent to the full architecture.

---

## Structural Topology

Represents broader patterns of connection among structural elements.

Topology emphasizes relationships and organization rather than only component inventory.

---

## Canonical Structural Representation

Provides a stabilized high-level representation of the research object based on the preceding structural analysis.

Canonical does not mean final or universally authoritative.

Within this repository, it indicates the representation currently accepted as the principal structural reference for that case.

---

## Structural Summary

Provides a compact orientation to the representation case.

It supports navigation without replacing the underlying structural assets.

---

## Representation Validation

Examines whether the resulting Structural Representation is sufficiently supported by the available evidence and whether significant distortion has been introduced during representation.

Validation may examine questions such as:

- Were unsupported structures introduced?
- Were important dependencies omitted?
- Was hierarchy inferred where only coordination exists?
- Were source-specific terms incorrectly generalized?
- Were functional and structural relationships confused?
- Does the Canonical Structural Representation remain traceable to the underlying evidence?

Validation is part of the research process rather than a cosmetic final check.

# Representative Resource Curation

World Model currently makes the preparation stage particularly visible.

Representative Resource Curation addresses a problem that precedes Structural Representation:

> **What evidence base should represent the research object before structural analysis begins?**

Its responsibilities may include:

```text
Candidate Resources
        ↓
Resource Evaluation
        ↓
Representative Resource Selection
        ↓
Selection Rationale
        ↓
Readiness Assessment
        ↓
Handover Package
```

This process reduces the risk that Structural Representation is constructed from an arbitrary or unexamined collection of materials.

However, Representative Resource Curation and Structural Representation remain distinct responsibilities.

Conceptually:

```text
Representative Resource Curator
              │
              ▼
        Evidence Package
              │
          HANDOVER
              │
              ▼
Structural Representation Process
```

This boundary should be preserved.

# Case Independence

Each representation case should first be allowed to stand independently.

The repository therefore follows the principle:

> **Representation integrity precedes comparative utility.**

A case should not be redesigned merely because another case has a different structure.

For example:

```text
SOAR Structure
      ≠
ACIM Structure
      ≠
World Model Structure
      ≠
AI Textbook Structure
```

The purpose of Structural Representation is precisely to preserve such differences while making each structure sufficiently explicit for later observation.

# Representation and Comparison

Structural Representation and Comparative Research are separate research responsibilities.

```text
Research Object A
        ↓
Representation A
        │
        │
        ├──────────────┐
        │              │
        ▼              ▼
   preserved      Comparative
   independently    Interface
                       ▲
        ▲              │
        │              │
Representation B ──────┘
        ↑
Research Object B
```

Comparison should therefore begin from representations that have already achieved sufficient internal stability.

The comparative process may identify:

- correspondence,
- non-correspondence,
- functional similarity,
- structural difference,
- hierarchical difference,
- topological difference,
- or previously unobserved relationships.

None of these outcomes should be predetermined during representation.

---

# Compare Representations, Not Resources

The repository adopts the following comparative discipline:

> **Compare Representations, not Resources.**

Raw resources may differ radically in:

- vocabulary,
- document format,
- historical context,
- domain conventions,
- level of abstraction,
- and explanatory style.

Direct comparison of raw resources can therefore produce superficial or misleading correspondence.

Structural Representation provides an intermediate research interface:

```text
Raw Resources A
      ↓
Structural Representation A
              │
              ▼
       Structural Comparison
              ▲
              │
Structural Representation B
      ↑
Raw Resources B
```

This does not eliminate interpretation.

It makes the basis of interpretation more explicit and structurally inspectable.

# Structural Diversity

The four current cases should not be interpreted as instances of a single known architecture.

Their structural diversity is itself research evidence.

The repository therefore preserves variation in:

- internal organization,
- representation history,
- preparation requirements,
- structural granularity,
- validation procedures,
- and developmental maturity.

Structural consistency at the repository level should not be confused with structural uniformity among research objects.

---

# Developmental Status

The current cases have not necessarily reached the same developmental stage.

Some cases originated before the current Structural Representation process became explicit.

Others were developed after representation procedures had become more structured.

World Model additionally exposes the Representative Resource Curation stage within the case itself.

This creates an important distinction:

```text
Same Research Domain
        ≠
Same Formation History
        ≠
Same Representation Maturity
```

The repository preserves these differences rather than rewriting earlier cases to create artificial historical uniformity.

# Research Value of Multiple Cases

Multiple Structural Representation cases make two forms of observation possible.

## Object-Level Observation

Each case allows examination of the structure of a particular research object.

```text
Research Object
      ↓
Structural Representation
      ↓
Object-Level Structural Understanding
```

## Methodological Observation

Across multiple cases, it becomes possible to observe the Structural Representation process itself.

```text
Case A ──┐
Case B ──┤
Case C ──┼──► Cross-Case Methodological Observation
Case D ──┘
```

This second form of observation should remain evidence-driven.

Repeated patterns should not automatically be treated as universal methodology.

# Current Structural Interpretation

At the present stage, `10-structural-representations/` can be understood as:

> **a collection of independently constructed Structural Representation cases that together provide both research interfaces for comparison and an empirical basis for observing the development of Structural Representation itself.**

This dual function is important.

The domain preserves representations of research objects while also preserving evidence about how Structural Representation behaves across heterogeneous cases.

# Boundary Conditions

This domain does **not** assume that:

- every research object has the same architecture,
- every representation must contain identical files,
- every representation follows the same formation history,
- Structural Skeleton automatically determines later maps,
- topology is always the final explanatory form,
- Canonical Structural Representation is permanently fixed,
- Representative Resource Curation is already validated as a universal prerequisite,
- or cross-case similarity proves theoretical equivalence.

Structural Representation should remain responsive to the evidence of the individual research object.
