# Case 001 — No Comparative Research

## Status

**Historical Marker**

---

## Purpose

Case 001 predates the emergence of Comparative Research within this Research Program.

At this stage, the primary activity was the development of Structural Representation rather than comparison between multiple Structural Representations.

Therefore, Case 001 should not be interpreted as a Comparative Research case.

---

## Why This Case Is Retained

This directory is intentionally retained to preserve:

* historical continuity of the Case sequence;
* traceability to the earlier Research Space in the Hub Repository;
* the developmental transition from Structural Representation to Comparative Research.

The Case numbering is therefore preserved even though no Comparative Research was conducted in Case 001.

---

## Historical Position

The developmental sequence should be understood as:

```text
Case 001
Structural Representation
        ↓
Case 002
Structural Representation
        ↓
Case 003
First emergence of Comparative Research
```

Case 003 represents the transition point at which completed Structural Representations began to function as reusable inputs for comparative research.
