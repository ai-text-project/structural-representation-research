# Case 002 — No Comparative Research

## Status

**Historical Marker**

---

## Purpose

Case 002 predates the emergence of Comparative Research within this Research Program.

Like Case 001, its primary role belonged to the development of Structural Representation rather than comparison between multiple Structural Representations.

Therefore, Case 002 should not be interpreted as a Comparative Research case.

---

## Why This Case Is Retained

This directory is intentionally retained to preserve:

* historical continuity of the Case sequence;
* traceability to the earlier Research Space in the Hub Repository;
* the developmental transition from Structural Representation to Comparative Research.

Removing Case 002 from the sequence would obscure the historical relationship between the earlier Structural Representation cases and the later emergence of Comparative Research.

---

## Historical Position

The developmental sequence should be understood as:

```text
Case 001
Structural Representation
        ↓
Case 002
Structural Representation
        ↓
Case 003
First emergence of Comparative Research
```

Case 002 therefore represents the final pre-comparative stage immediately preceding the emergence of Comparative Research in Case 003.

---

No Comparative Research assets are expected to be stored here unless future historical reconstruction requires additional documentation.
