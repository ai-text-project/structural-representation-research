# Structural Correspondence

**Case:** 004 — World Model + SOAR (Provisional)

**Current Stage:** Comparative Observation

---

# Purpose

This document records the structural correspondences observed between the accepted Structural Representation Packages of the World Model and SOAR.

The objective is not to compare terminology or theoretical claims.

Instead, the present observation focuses on organizational structure after both research objects have independently completed the Structural Representation process.

---

# Observation Principle

The present comparison follows the methodological principle:

> **Compare Representations, not Resources.**

Accordingly,

the comparison is conducted between:

- World Model Representation Package

and

- SOAR Representation Package.

The original research resources remain outside the scope of this document.

---

# Structural Observation

The accepted Representation Packages suggest that both research objects are organized as integrated structural systems rather than collections of independent concepts.

In both cases, structural organization appears to emerge through relationships among multiple architectural components.

Neither Representation presents its principal elements as isolated entities.

Instead, both describe organized structures whose components participate in broader organizational processes.

---

# Initial Structural Correspondence

At the present stage, the following structural correspondences are observed.

## Organization over Enumeration

Both Representation Packages prioritize organizational structure rather than simple lists of concepts.

---

## Structural Progression

Both Representation Packages exhibit developmental progression across multiple organizational levels.

Although the internal architectures differ,

each Representation organizes its structural components into coherent developmental sequences.

---

## Internal Dependency

Both structures demonstrate explicit dependency relationships among their organizational components.

Structural units appear to support, constrain, or prepare subsequent structural units.

---

## Hierarchical Organization

Both Representation Packages contain multiple structural layers.

These layers contribute different organizational responsibilities while remaining integrated within the overall architecture.

---

## Canonical Integration

Both Representation Packages conclude with a Canonical Structural Representation.

The Canonical Representation functions as an integrated structural interface rather than as an additional theoretical contribution.

This provides a common structural basis for comparative observation.

---

# Observed Differences

Structural correspondence does not imply structural identity.

At the present stage,

important differences are also preserved.

For example,

- different organizational objectives,
- different architectural origins,
- different representational emphasis,

remain observable.

These differences are regarded as research evidence rather than inconsistencies.

---

# Current Assessment

The present observation indicates that both Representation Packages satisfy the methodological conditions required for structural comparison.

Structural correspondence is therefore considered observable.

However,

the precise functional significance of these correspondences remains outside the scope of the present document.

Functional relationships will be examined separately.
