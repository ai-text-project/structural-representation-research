# Functional Correspondence

**Case:** 004 — World Model + SOAR (Provisional)

**Current Stage:** Comparative Observation

---

# Purpose

This document records the functional correspondences observed between the accepted Representation Packages of the World Model and SOAR.

The objective is not to determine whether identical computational mechanisms exist.

Instead, the present observation examines whether comparable organizational functions emerge within the respective Structural Representations.

Functional correspondence is therefore investigated at the level of architectural organization rather than implementation.

---

# Observation Principle

The present comparison follows the methodological principle:

> **Compare Representations, not Resources.**

Accordingly,

functional observation is conducted between the accepted Representation Packages.

No attempt is made to compare algorithms, implementation details, or theoretical terminology directly.

---

# Functional Observation

The accepted Representation Packages indicate that both research objects organize multiple structural components into coordinated functional systems.

Individual structural elements do not operate independently.

Instead,

their functions emerge through relationships within larger organizational architectures.

---

# Initial Functional Correspondence

At the present stage, the following functional correspondences are observed.

## Coordinated Organization

Both Representation Packages describe organizational systems in which multiple structural components cooperate rather than function independently.

---

## Sequential Function

In both representations,

organizational functions appear to unfold through ordered developmental progression.

Earlier structural units establish conditions that support subsequent organizational functions.

---

## Dependency-Based Function

Functional organization depends upon structural dependency.

The observed functions are therefore relational rather than isolated.

---

## Integration

Both Representation Packages ultimately integrate multiple organizational functions into coherent structural systems.

Integration appears as an organizational outcome rather than an independent functional module.

---

## Representation as Functional Interface

In both cases,

the Canonical Structural Representation serves as the primary interface through which organizational functions become observable.

The Representation therefore functions as an analytical interface rather than merely a descriptive summary.

---

# Functional Differences

The present observation also preserves important functional differences.

For example,

- different organizational purposes,
- different functional priorities,
- different architectural scopes,

remain observable.

These differences should not be interpreted as methodological inconsistency.

Instead,

they provide valuable comparative evidence concerning how different research objects organize their internal functional relationships.

---

# Current Assessment

The present comparison suggests that functional correspondence is observable at the organizational level.

However,

the observation does not imply that equivalent computational mechanisms or theoretical constructs exist.

The present document records only organizational correspondences that emerge from the accepted Representation Packages.
