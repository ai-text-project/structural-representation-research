# Hierarchical Projection

**Case:** 004 — World Model + SOAR (Provisional)

**Current Stage:** Comparative Observation

---

# Purpose

This document investigates the hierarchical organization observable across the accepted Representation Packages of the World Model and SOAR.

The objective is not to construct a unified architecture.

Instead,

the present observation projects both Structural Representations onto a common organizational perspective in order to examine hierarchical relationships.

Hierarchical Projection therefore represents an observational process rather than a theoretical integration.

---

# Observation Principle

The present document follows the methodological principle:

> **Compare Representations, not Resources.**

Hierarchical Projection is performed only after:

- Structural Correspondence

and

- Functional Correspondence

have already been observed.

Accordingly,

the present document investigates higher-level organizational relationships emerging from the accepted Representation Packages.

---

# Hierarchical Observation

The accepted Representation Packages indicate that both research objects organize their structural components across multiple levels of abstraction.

Individual structural units participate within broader organizational systems rather than existing independently.

Hierarchy therefore appears as an organizational property of the overall Representation.

---

# Initial Hierarchical Projection

The following hierarchical observations are currently supported.

## Multi-Level Organization

Both Representation Packages exhibit multiple organizational levels.

Structural components contribute different responsibilities depending upon their position within the overall architecture.

---

## Progressive Organization

Organizational responsibility appears to develop progressively.

Lower-level structures establish conditions for higher-level organizational functions.

The resulting hierarchy reflects organizational dependency rather than simple accumulation.

---

## Organizational Integration

Higher organizational levels integrate relationships established by lower structural levels.

Integration therefore emerges through structural organization rather than through isolated architectural modules.

---

## Canonical Projection

The Canonical Structural Representation serves as the highest organizational projection within each Representation Package.

Rather than introducing new architectural elements,

the Canonical Representation organizes previously observed structural relationships into an integrated organizational view.

---

# Hierarchical Differences

Hierarchical correspondence does not imply identical organizational architecture.

Observable differences remain.

For example,

- different organizational emphasis,
- different structural granularity,
- different abstraction strategies,

continue to characterize each Representation.

These differences are preserved as comparative evidence.

---

# Current Assessment

The present observation indicates that hierarchical organization is observable across both Representation Packages.

The observed hierarchy concerns organizational abstraction rather than theoretical equivalence.

The present document therefore records hierarchical relationships without attempting theoretical unification.

---

# Methodological Significance

The present observation suggests that Hierarchical Projection provides an additional observational layer beyond Structural Correspondence and Functional Correspondence.

Accordingly,

comparative observation may be understood as progressively examining:

```text
Structural Correspondence
            ↓
Functional Correspondence
            ↓
Hierarchical Projection
```

Each stage increases the level of organizational abstraction while preserving the conceptual independence of the original Representation Packages.
