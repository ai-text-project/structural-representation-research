# Purpose

This document defines the comparison object, representation boundary, evidence conditions, and execution provenance for the new Case 005 comparative package.

The purpose of this package is not to compare the original World Model and ACIM source materials directly.

Instead, the comparison is conducted between two independently reconstructed and frozen Structural Representations:

- **Frozen Projection A — World Model**
- **Frozen Projection B — ACIM**

The comparative object is therefore:

> **Frozen Projection A × Frozen Projection B**

This distinction is fundamental to the interpretation of all subsequent comparative artifacts in this package.

---

# Comparison Scope

The scope of Case 005 is the structural comparison of two independently reconstructed representations.

The comparison examines, where supported:

- Structural Units
- Relations

- Hierarchy

- Flow
- Feedback / Recurrence

- Different Structural Responsibility
- Different Dependency Responsibility
- Different Transformation Responsibility
- Different Closure Condition
- Undetermined
- Insufficient Evidence

The purpose of these categories is to preserve structural differences rather than force symmetry between the two representations.

# Comparison Objects

## Projection A — World Model

Projection A is an independently reconstructed Structural Representation of the provisional World Model.

Its stable represented core includes:

- World
- Representation
- Understanding
- Prediction

Within the frozen representation:

- Representation functions as the structural and conceptual center.
- Understanding depends on Representation.
- Prediction depends on Representation and Understanding.
- Search, Planning, Decision-Making, and Action remain possible architectural extensions rather than stabilized members of the represented core.

Projection A therefore preserves a distinction between:

> **Stable Core**

and

> **Open / Unresolved Architectural Extension**

Projection A was independently reviewed and frozen before comparative observation began.

# Representation Package Boundary

The comparison in this package is performed at the level of Structural Representation.

It does not directly compare:

- original source documents;
- terminology alone;
- theological claims;
- scientific claims;
- conceptual labels in isolation;
- surface semantic similarity.

The principal comparative concern is how independently reconstructed structures organize:

- units;
- relations;
- dependencies;
- responsibilities;
- transformations;
- hierarchy;
- topology;
- boundaries;
- closure.

Accordingly:

> **Structural Correspondence ≠ Conceptual Identity**

and:

> **Functional Similarity ≠ Structural Equivalence**

The existence of similarly named concepts or apparently similar sequences is not sufficient evidence of structural correspondence.

# Restabilized Comparison Position

Following verification, the comparison was restabilized at three distinguishable levels.

## Representation / Formal Level

Meaningful formal correspondence is present in areas such as:

- relation differentiation;
- responsibility separation;
- boundary discipline;
- preservation of non-collapse distinctions.

However, the origin of this similarity is not fully determined.

It may reflect properties of the represented research objects, properties of their Structural Representations, properties of the Representation Methodology, or some combination of these.

---

## Abstract Organizational Level

Limited and qualified correspondence is supported in areas such as:

- differentiated responsibilities;
- structured dependencies;
- prior structural conditions constraining downstream responsibilities.

These similarities do not establish identical dependency structures or identical functional responsibilities.

---

## Object-Structural / Topological Level

Material Non-Correspondence is strongly supported.

Projection A is characterized by a comparatively compact, Representation-centered and Prediction-directed stable core with an open architectural extension boundary.

Projection B is characterized by a Layer-anchored, developmentally organized, Recovery / Reintegration-oriented, operationally maintained, multi-dimensional architecture with explicit representational closure.

These differences are preserved as substantive comparative results.

---

# Representation-Level and Object-Level Distinction

The comparison revealed a necessary distinction among:

```text
Research Object Structure
        ≠
Representation Structure
        ≠
Representation Methodology
```

These levels must not be collapsed.

Some formal similarities observed between Projection A and Projection B may reflect properties of the Structural Representation process rather than correspondence between the underlying research objects themselves.

Accordingly:

> **Representation-Level Correspondence ≠ Research-Object-Level Correspondence**

and:

> **Methodology-Induced Similarity ≠ Object-Level Structural Correspondence**

The current Frozen Representations do not fully determine the provenance of the strongest formal similarities.

That question therefore remains bounded rather than resolved by assumption.
