# 1. Functional Responsibility in Projection A — World Model

Projection A contains a compact set of differentiated functional responsibilities.

Its stable represented core consists of:

- World
- Representation
- Understanding
- Prediction

The functional organization is not equivalent to an execution pipeline.

However, differentiated responsibilities are identifiable.

---

## 1.1 World

Within Projection A, World functions as the external structural condition relative to which Representation is formed.

It is not treated as equivalent to Representation.

The distinction:

```text
World
  ≠
Representation
```

is therefore functionally significant.

World provides the represented external domain.

Representation provides the internal structural representation of that domain.

The comparison must preserve this boundary.

---

## 1.2 Representation

Representation has the strongest central functional responsibility within Projection A.

Its responsibility is to maintain an internal structural representation of the external environment or world.

It therefore occupies a position that is simultaneously:

- structurally central;
- prerequisite to Understanding;
- prerequisite to Prediction.

The frozen dependency structure supports:

```text
World
  ↓
Representation
```

followed by:

```text
Representation
      ↓
Understanding
```

and:

```text
Representation
      +
Understanding
      ↓
Prediction
```

Representation is therefore not merely one functional module among equivalent peers.

It is a prerequisite structural responsibility for downstream cognitive functions represented in the stable core.

---

## 1.3 Understanding

Understanding performs a distinct responsibility from Representation.

Its responsibility is to transform or interpret represented states into coherent semantic understanding.

This responsibility depends on Representation.

Therefore:

```text
Representation
      ↓
Understanding
```

does not mean:

```text
Representation
      =
Understanding
```

The functional distinction is essential.

Representation maintains internal structure.

Understanding operates upon that represented structure to produce coherent semantic interpretation.

---

## 1.4 Prediction

Prediction performs another differentiated responsibility.

Its function is to generate possible future states or state transitions based on the internal representation and the understanding derived from it.

The frozen dependency relation is:

```text
Representation
      +
Understanding
      ↓
Prediction
```

Prediction is therefore downstream from both.

However:

> **Dependency ≠ Temporal Execution**

The relation does not establish an exact computational or temporal sequence.

---

## 1.5 Possible Functional Extensions

Projection A also preserves possible architectural extensions:

- Search
- Planning
- Decision-Making
- Action

Their precise status is unresolved.

They may represent additional downstream responsibilities in a broader architecture, but they are not stabilized members of the Frozen Projection A core.

Therefore:

```text
Possible Functional Extension
        ≠
Established Functional Responsibility
```

No functional correspondence with Projection B is inferred from these unresolved extensions.
