> **Projection A contains layer differentiation, but the Frozen Projection does not justify treating that differentiation as a fully established hierarchical architecture.**

# 2. Conceptual Layer — Projection A

The Conceptual Layer contains:

- World
- Representation

The relation between these units is structurally important.

World and Representation must remain distinct:

```text
World
  ≠
Representation
```

World functions as the external structural condition.

Representation functions as the internal structural representation of that condition.

The placement of these units in a common conceptual layer does not mean that they perform the same responsibility.

The supported observation is more limited:

> They belong to the conceptual organization of the stable represented core.
