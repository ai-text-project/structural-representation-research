# 39. Overall Restabilized Judgment

The new Case 005 supports meaningful structural correspondence between World Model and ACIM only under carefully bounded conditions.

The strongest correspondence occurs at the formal level of Structural Representation.

Both representations preserve differentiated units, typed relations, dependency distinctions, functional responsibilities, boundaries, and non-collapse invariants.

At a higher abstract organizational level, both also support a limited pattern in which prior structural conditions constrain downstream responsibilities.

However, these similarities do not extend into object-level structural identity.

The object-level architectures remain materially different.

Projection A is:

> **Representation-centered, Prediction-directed, comparatively compact, and architecturally open beyond its stable core.**

Projection B is:

> **Foundation-anchored, differentiation-generating, developmentally organized, Recovery / Reintegration-oriented, operationally maintained, Layer-anchored, multi-dimensional, and explicitly stabilized as a Structural Representation.**

The strongest object-specific features do not map directly across the two representations.

In particular:

```text
Representation-centeredness A
        ≠
Layer-anchored organization B
```

```text
Prediction-directedness A
        ≠
Recovery / Operational orientation B
```

```text
Open Extension A
        ≠
Cross-Layer Reusability within
Stabilized Architecture B
```

```text
Provisional Layering A
        ≠
Explicit Five-Layer Hierarchy B
```

Therefore, the Restabilized Comparative Judgment is:

> **Meaningful Representation-Level Formal Correspondence, Limited Abstract Organizational Correspondence, and Material Object-Level Structural / Topological Non-Correspondence.**
