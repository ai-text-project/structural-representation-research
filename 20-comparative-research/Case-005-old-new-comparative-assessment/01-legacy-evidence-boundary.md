# Purpose

This document defines the evidence boundary governing the comparative assessment between:

> **Old Case 005 — World Model × ACIM**

and

> **New Independent Case 005 — World Model × ACIM**

The purpose of this artifact is not to determine whether the Old and New Cases agree.

The purpose is not to evaluate which Case is superior.

The purpose is not to reconstruct missing portions of the Legacy Case.

Its responsibility is narrower:

> **to establish exactly what Legacy evidence exists, what does not exist, what can legitimately be compared, and what cannot be inferred from the incomplete formation state of the Old Case.**

This boundary must remain stable throughout all subsequent Old × New comparative artifacts.

---

# 1. Comparative Context

The Old and New Cases concern the same broad comparative research pairing:

```text

     ×
ACIM
```

However, they were formed under materially different research-process conditions.

The Old Case was an earlier comparative attempt.

The New Case was intentionally formed without using the substantive content of the Old Case as a target answer.

Only after the New Case had reached its own restabilized comparative judgment and repository projection was the Old Case disclosed for Legacy intake.

The resulting Old × New assessment therefore compares:

> **an actually formed Legacy comparative object**

with

> **an independently formed and pre-Legacy-frozen New comparative object.**

# 11. Meaning of Independence

The independence claim used in this assessment is bounded.

It means:

> **The substantive findings of the Old Case were not used as target answers during formation of the New comparative judgment.**

It does not mean:

- the two Cases used unrelated research objects;
- the two Cases had independent empirical datasets;
- the underlying World Model and ACIM materials were unrelated;
- the broader Structural Representation research environment was unrelated;
- shared methodological architecture could not influence both Cases.

Therefore:

```text
Independent Analytical Formation
        ≠
Independent Source Evidence
```

and:

```text
Independent Case Formation
        ≠
Methodological Independence
```

This distinction becomes particularly important when evaluating apparent Old × New convergence.

# 15. Stage-Unavailable Boundary

When the New Case contains an observation belonging to a stage that the Legacy Case never reached, the absence of a corresponding Legacy observation must be classified as:

> **STAGE-UNAVAILABLE**

rather than:

> **New-only**

For example, later New Case observations concerning:

- higher-order structural topology;
- closure architecture;
- recursive verification;
- methodology-induced similarity;
- explicit feedback / recursion boundaries;
- restabilized final judgment;

cannot automatically be interpreted as discoveries that the Legacy Case failed to make.

The Legacy process did not reach the corresponding analytical stage.

Therefore:

```text
STAGE-UNAVAILABLE
        ≠
NEW-ONLY
```

# 17. When Direct Comparison Is Permitted

Direct Old × New comparison is justified when the Legacy Case had already formed a substantive judgment within the relevant analytical responsibility.

Examples include Legacy judgments concerning:

- progressive structural development;
- dependency;
- hierarchy;
- canonical integration;
- World Model functional progression;
- ACIM organizational development;
- organizational integration;
- structural correspondence;
- functional correspondence.

Because the Legacy Case actually formed judgments in these areas, they may legitimately be compared with the independently formed New judgments.

In such cases, the incomplete state of later Legacy artifacts does not eliminate the earlier formed evidence.

---

# 18. When Direct Comparison Is Not Permitted

Direct comparison is not justified merely because the New Case contains a later observation for which no Legacy statement exists.

Where the Legacy process never reached the corresponding analytical responsibility, the correct classification is:

> **STAGE-UNAVAILABLE**

or, where provenance remains unclear:

> **LEGACY-UNDETERMINED**

The assessment must not convert an unformed Legacy stage into an inferred Legacy position.

---

# 19. Non-Reproduction Boundary

Another distinction is required for observations that were explicitly formed in the Legacy Case but were not independently reproduced in the New Case.

```text
NON-REPRODUCED
        ≠
FALSIFIED
```

A Legacy observation may fail to reappear in the New reconstruction because of:

- different granularity;
- different evidence threshold;
- different reconstruction boundary;
- New omission;
- Legacy over-integration;
- another unresolved explanatory factor.

Non-Reproduction therefore records a comparative fact:

> **the same stable observation was not independently re-formed under the New Case conditions.**

It does not by itself determine which formation is correct.

---

# 20. Reclassification Boundary

A Legacy observation may also remain recognizable while receiving a different evidential status in the New Case.

This is classified as:

> **RECLASSIFIED**

rather than:

> **Legacy Error**

For example, an observation initially treated broadly as Correspondence may later be separated into:

```text
Formal Correspondence

Qualified Organizational Correspondence

Different Structural Responsibility

Material Non-Correspondence

Undetermined
```

Such a change means that the evidential location of the observation changed.

It does not automatically mean that the earlier observation was invalid.

Therefore:

```text
RECLASSIFIED
        ≠
LEGACY ERROR
```

---

# 21. Preserved Boundary

Likewise:

```text
PRESERVED
        ≠
VALIDATED
```

If an Old observation independently reappears in the New Case, that is evidence of analytical convergence under the present conditions.

It does not establish:

- empirical truth;
- theoretical validity;
- scientific correctness;
- universal structural correspondence;
- methodological superiority.

Preservation is a comparative status, not external validation.

# 22. Representation-Level and Object-Level Evidence

The current assessment distinguishes several possible locations of apparent correspondence.


These levels must not be collapsed.

A correspondence visible in both repository packages may arise because the underlying research objects share a structural property.

But it may also arise because both objects were externalized through a shared representation methodology.

Therefore:

```text
Representation-Level Correspondence
        ≠
Research-Object-Level Correspondence
```

This provenance problem remains an explicit evidence boundary.

# 32. New-only Classification Constraint

The category:

> **NEW-ONLY**

requires unusually strong evidence.

It should only be used if:

1. the Legacy Case reached the relevant analytical responsibility;
2. the Legacy evidence is sufficiently formed to establish what was considered;
3. the relevant observation is absent despite that opportunity;
4. the absence cannot reasonably be explained by stage incompleteness;
5. alternative provenance explanations have been considered.

Under the present evidence boundary, strong New-only findings are not generally established.

This prevents:

```text
New contains X
+
Old does not contain X
        ↓
X is a New-only discovery
```

from becoming an automatic inference.

---

# 33. Legacy-Undetermined Classification

Where New contains an observation but the Legacy formation state does not allow a justified judgment about whether the Old process would have formed, rejected, or revised it, the appropriate status is:

> **LEGACY-UNDETERMINED**

This category preserves uncertainty rather than converting incomplete history into evidence.

It is especially important for observations emerging from later verification responsibilities.

# 43. Boundary Judgment

The incomplete formation of the Legacy Case does not prevent meaningful comparison.

It changes the kind of comparison that is justified.

The valid comparison is not:

> **complete Old Case × complete New Case**

but:

> **formed Legacy observations × independently formed New judgments.**

Where both Cases reached the relevant analytical responsibility, direct comparison is permitted.

Where the Legacy Case did not reach that responsibility, the appropriate status is Stage-Unavailable or Legacy-Undetermined.

This distinction protects the comparison from converting historical incompleteness into artificial divergence.
