# Purpose

This document records the observations formed in the Old Case 005 that remained substantively recognizable after comparison with the independently reconstructed and pre-Legacy-frozen New Case 005.

Its purpose is not to demonstrate that the Old and New Cases reached the same overall conclusion.

Its purpose is not to treat repeated observations as validation.

Its purpose is not to maximize the number of correspondences between the two Cases.

Its responsibility is narrower:

> **to identify what remained stable across the Old and New analytical formations, while distinguishing methodological, representational, analytical, object-structural, and difference-preserving forms of convergence.**

The central question is therefore not:

> How similar are the Old and New Cases?

It is:

> **Which observations survived independent re-formation, and at what evidential level did that convergence occur?**

# 2. Meaning of Preserved

Within this artifact:

> **PRESERVED**

means that an observation explicitly formed in the Legacy Case remains substantively supported or recognizable in the New Case.

It does not mean that every aspect of the Legacy formulation remains unchanged.

A preserved observation may later receive:

- narrower scope;
- more explicit responsibility separation;
- stronger evidence boundaries;
- different provenance classification;
- more precise terminology.

Therefore:

```text
PRESERVED
        ≠
IDENTICAL WORDING
```

and:

```text
PRESERVED
        ≠
UNCHANGED GRANULARITY
```

and:

```text
PRESERVED
        ≠
VALIDATED
```

Preservation is a comparative relationship between two analytical formations.

# 3. Meaning of Convergence

