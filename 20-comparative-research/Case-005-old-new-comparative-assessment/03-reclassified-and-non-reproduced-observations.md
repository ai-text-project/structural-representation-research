# Purpose

This document records the Legacy Case 005 observations whose evidential status changed when compared with the independently reconstructed and pre-Legacy-frozen New Case 005.

Its primary responsibility is to distinguish two different comparative outcomes:

> **RECLASSIFIED**

and

> **NON-REPRODUCED**

A Reclassified observation remains recognizable across Old and New, but its valid scope, level, responsibility, or evidence strength changes.

A Non-Reproduced observation was explicitly formed in the Legacy Case but was not independently re-formed as the same stable observation in the New Case.

Neither category means that the Legacy observation was false.

Accordingly:

```text
RECLASSIFIED
        ≠
LEGACY ERROR
```

and:

```text
NON-REPRODUCED
        ≠
FALSIFIED
```

This artifact therefore asks:

> **Which Legacy correspondences survived only after being localized, decomposed, or qualified, and which Legacy observation did not independently reappear in the New reconstruction?**

# 3. Meaning of Reclassification

Within this assessment:

> **RECLASSIFIED**

means that the New independent reconstruction does not simply reproduce the Legacy observation at its original breadth, but enough of the observation remains recognizable to support a more bounded judgment.

Reclassification may involve:

```text
Broad
→ Limited

Object-Level
→ Representation-Level

→ Different Responsibility

Strong Correspondence
→ Qualified Correspondence

Correspondence
→ Formal Correspondence + Material Non-Correspondence

Single Judgment

```

Reclassification preserves historical continuity while preventing correspondence inflation.

---

# 4. Reclassification Is Not Correction

The following inference is prohibited:

```text
Old Judgment
        ↓
New Reclassification
        ↓
Old was wrong
```

The valid statement is narrower:

```text
Old Judgment
        ↓
Independent Re-challenge
        ↓
Same breadth not sustained
        ↓
More bounded status assigned
```

The New Case may use:

- different reconstruction granularity;
- different evidence thresholds;
- explicit responsibility separation;
- explicit Freeze controls;
- higher-order comparison;
- recursive verification.

Therefore a changed classification does not by itself establish that the earlier judgment was invalid.

# 5. Meaning of Non-Reproduction

Within this artifact:

> **NON-REPRODUCED**

means that an explicitly formed Legacy observation was not independently reconstructed as the same stable architecture or judgment in the New Case.

This is stronger than:

```text
No Legacy Counterpart
```

because the Legacy observation actually existed.

But it remains weaker than:

```text
Falsified
```

because competing explanations may remain unresolved.
