# Part 1 — Formation Boundary and Core Asymmetry

## 1. Purpose

This document records the **formation asymmetry** and **stage asymmetry** between Old Case 005 and New Independent Case 005.

Its purpose is not to determine which Case is superior.

Its purpose is not to infer that the more complete Case is more correct.

Its purpose is to preserve the historical difference between the analytical responsibilities actually formed in Old Case 005 and those formed in New Independent Case 005.

The governing invariant is:

> **Different Formation Architecture ≠ Different Truth Value**

The two Cases reached materially different stages of analytical formation.

That difference must remain visible throughout the Old × New comparative assessment.

## 6. Stage Asymmetry

Formation Asymmetry produces **Stage Asymmetry**.

Stage Asymmetry occurs when one Case reaches an analytical responsibility that the other Case does not reach.

Suppose New contains observation X while Old contains no corresponding observation.

Several explanations remain possible:

    New Observation X
            ↓
    No corresponding Old judgment

may mean:

    A. Old formed a materially different judgment.

or:

    B. Old formed a broader or less differentiated judgment.

or:

    C. Old never reached the analytical stage responsible for forming X.

These conditions are not equivalent.

Only A or B permits ordinary direct comparison.

Condition C requires preservation of Stage Asymmetry.
