This document records the **Recursive Verification** performed after the initial Old × New comparative assessment.

> **Comparison Result ≠ Verification Result**

- theoretical truth;
- source-level completeness;
- scientific confirmation;
- superiority of one research object;
- superiority of one analytical instance.

Therefore:

> **Recursive Verification ≠ External Validation**

> **Survived Verification ≠ Proven Correct**

# 6. Verification Objective

The verification was designed to detect whether the provisional Old × New assessment contained:

- correspondence inflation;
- forced symmetry;
- excessive non-correspondence;
- methodology-induced similarity;
- granularity artifacts;
- stage-confounded judgments;
- formation-completeness bias;
- unsupported novelty claims;
- unsupported falsification claims;
- over-reading of the frozen evidence.

The objective was not to maximize agreement or disagreement.

The objective was:

> **Judgment Stability under Alternative Explanation.**

---

# 7. Governing Verification Principle

Every material provisional judgment was subject to the question:

> **Could the same evidence reasonably support a materially different classification?**

Where the answer was yes, the alternative had to be preserved.

Where the alternative materially weakened the original judgment, the judgment had to be qualified or reclassified.

Where the alternative could not be resolved, the result had to remain:

> **UNDETERMINED**

---

# 8. Verification Outcomes

The available verification outcomes were:

- **PRESERVE**
- **QUALIFY**
- **RECLASSIFY**
- **WITHDRAW**
- **UNDETERMINED**
- **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE**

These outcomes refer to provisional comparative judgments.

They do not refer to the truth or falsity of World Model or ACIM.

---

# 9. Preserve

**PRESERVE** means:

> The provisional judgment remained materially supported after the relevant alternative or reverse check.

It does not mean:

> The judgment has been externally validated.

---

# 10. Qualify

**QUALIFY** means:

> The central observation remains supported, but its scope, strength, provenance, or interpretation must be narrowed.

Qualification is not rejection.

It is boundary formation.

---

# 11. Reclassify

**RECLASSIFY** means:

> The evidence still supports a material observation, but the original judgment category was too strong, too broad, or located at the wrong analytical level.

Example structure:

    Strong Object-Level
    Correspondence

            ↓
    Verification

            ↓
    Strong Representation-Level
    Formal Correspondence

This preserves the observation while changing its evidential location.

---

# 12. Withdraw

**WITHDRAW** means:

> A provisional classification could not survive verification and should no longer be retained in that form.

Withdrawal is a positive verification result.

It demonstrates that Recursive Verification can change rather than merely confirm prior judgments.

---

# 13. Undetermined

**UNDETERMINED** means:

> Multiple materially plausible explanations remain and the current frozen evidence cannot discriminate among them.

Undetermined is not analytical failure.

It is an evidence-boundary judgment.
