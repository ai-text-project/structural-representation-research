# Comparative Principle

The present case adopts the following methodological principle:

> **Compare Representations, not Resources.**

Structural Representation functions as an intermediate research interface.

Each Representation Package preserves the internal organization of its corresponding research object while providing a common structural surface for comparative observation.

The purpose of comparison is therefore to investigate structural organization while preserving conceptual independence.

# Comparative Characteristics

Unlike previous comparative cases within the AI domain,

the present case compares Representation Packages originating from substantially different research domains.

Accordingly,

the primary objective is not to identify conceptual similarity.

Instead,

the comparison investigates whether organizational relationships become observable through Structural Representation despite substantial differences in terminology, theoretical assumptions, and research objectives.

The present case therefore provides an opportunity to examine the robustness of Structural Representation as a comparative research interface across heterogeneous knowledge systems.
