# Functional Correspondence

**Case:** 005 — World Model + ACIM (Provisional)

**Current Stage:** Comparative Observation

---

# Purpose

This document records the functional correspondences observed between the accepted Representation Packages of the World Model and ACIM.

The objective is not to compare computational mechanisms, psychological processes, or theological concepts.

Instead,

the present observation examines whether comparable organizational functions emerge within the respective Structural Representations.

Accordingly,

functional correspondence is investigated at the level of organizational responsibility rather than domain-specific implementation.

---

# Observation Principle

The present comparison follows the methodological principle:

> **Compare Representations, not Resources.**

Functional observation is therefore conducted exclusively between the accepted Representation Packages.

The original research resources remain conceptually independent throughout the comparison.

---

# Functional Observation

The accepted Representation Packages indicate that both research objects organize multiple structural components into coherent organizational systems.

Individual structural elements do not function independently.

Instead,

their organizational roles emerge through structural relationships established within the overall Representation.

The present observation therefore concerns organizational function rather than domain-specific functionality.

---

# Initial Functional Correspondence

At the present stage, the following functional correspondences are observed.

## Coordinated Organization

Both Representation Packages describe organizational systems whose structural components contribute to larger coordinated organizations.

No structural unit appears to exist in complete isolation.

---

## Sequential Organizational Function

In both representations,

organizational functions emerge progressively.

Earlier organizational structures establish conditions that support subsequent structural development.

---

## Dependency-Based Organization

The organizational roles of individual structural components depend upon their structural relationships.

Functional organization therefore appears to emerge through dependency rather than isolated operation.

---

## Organizational Integration

Both Representation Packages ultimately integrate multiple organizational roles into coherent structural organizations.

Integration appears as an emergent organizational property rather than as an independent structural element.

---

## Representation as Functional Interface

In both cases,

the Canonical Structural Representation serves as the principal interface through which organizational functions become observable.

Accordingly,

the Representation functions as an analytical organizational interface rather than merely a descriptive summary.

---

# Functional Differences

The present observation also preserves substantial functional differences.

For example,

- different organizational purposes,
- different representational domains,
- different structural objectives,

remain observable.

These differences are preserved as comparative evidence.

The present comparison therefore does not assume equivalent functional mechanisms.

---

# Current Assessment

The present observation suggests that functional correspondence is observable at the level of organizational responsibility.

However,

the observation does not imply equivalent theoretical, computational, or conceptual functions.

Instead,

the observed correspondences concern how organizational roles become structured within independently developed Representation Packages.

---

# Methodological Note

The present case further suggests that organizational function may represent a more stable comparative perspective than conceptual terminology when structurally different research domains are examined.

At the present stage,

this remains an observational hypothesis rather than a methodological conclusion.

Future comparative cases are expected to provide additional evidence concerning this possibility.
