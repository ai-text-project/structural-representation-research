# Functional Correspondence

**Case:** 005 — World Model + ACIM (Provisional)

**Current Stage:** Comparative Observation

---

# Purpose

This document records the functional correspondences observed between the accepted Representation Packages of the World Model and ACIM.

The objective is not to compare computational mechanisms or theological concepts.

Instead,

the present observation examines how each Representation organizes the functional progression of its own structural components.

Accordingly,

the comparison concerns organizational function rather than conceptual equivalence.

---

# Functional Observation

Although the World Model and ACIM originate from substantially different research domains,

both Representation Packages organize their structures through progressive organizational relationships.

The present observation therefore examines how structural units contribute to the development of larger organizational systems.

---

# Observed Functional Correspondence

## Progressive Organization

The World Model Representation organizes its structural progression through:

```text
Representation
        ↓
Search
        ↓
Prediction
        ↓
Planning
        ↓
Action
```

Each organizational stage prepares the conditions for the next stage.

The overall organization therefore appears as a progressive functional architecture.

---

The ACIM Representation organizes its structural progression differently.

Rather than emphasizing computational processes,

its Representation progressively develops conceptual organization through multiple structural layers that culminate in an integrated Canonical Structural Representation.

Although the organizational content differs,

the Representation similarly exhibits progressive organizational development.

---

## Dependency Organization

In both Representation Packages,

later organizational structures depend upon relationships established by earlier structures.

Neither Representation presents its principal structures as independent organizational units.

Instead,

organizational dependency appears to provide continuity throughout the Representation.

---

## Organizational Integration

The World Model Representation ultimately integrates multiple computationally oriented structural components into a coherent organizational system.

The ACIM Representation similarly integrates multiple conceptual structural organizations into an overall structural architecture.

The resulting integrated organizations differ substantially in content,

yet both Representation Packages exhibit functional integration as the final organizational outcome.

---

## Canonical Representation as Functional Interface

Both Representation Packages conclude with Canonical Structural Representation.

Within the comparative process,

the Canonical Representation functions as the primary interface through which organizational functions become observable.

This shared organizational role supports comparative observation despite the substantial differences between the underlying research domains.

---

# Functional Differences

The observed organizational functions remain substantially different.

For example,

the World Model Representation primarily organizes computational intelligence.

The ACIM Representation primarily organizes conceptual and relational structures.

Accordingly,

functional correspondence concerns organizational structure rather than equivalent domain-specific functionality.

---

# Current Assessment

The present observation suggests that both Representation Packages organize structural development through progressive organizational dependency and subsequent organizational integration.

However,

the nature of the organizational functions differs substantially.

The present comparison therefore supports functional correspondence at the level of organizational architecture while preserving clear domain differences.
