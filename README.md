# Structural Representation Research

**Repository:** `structural-representation-research`  
**Status:** Active Research Repository  
**Primary Domain:** Structural Representation / Comparative Structural Research

---

# Purpose

This repository provides a dedicated research space for constructing, validating, comparing, and synthesizing structural representations of different knowledge systems, cognitive architectures, conceptual frameworks, and related research objects.

The repository is designed around a simple principle:

> **Represent structures before comparing them.**

Rather than beginning with terminology, surface descriptions, or direct conceptual comparison, each research object is first examined as a structure.

The resulting Structural Representations can then serve as common interfaces for comparative research.

---

# Research Orientation

The repository supports a research progression of:

```text
Research Object
      │
      ▼
Representative Resources
      │
      ▼
Structural Representation
      │
      ▼
Representation Validation
      │
      ▼
Comparative Research
      │
      ▼

      │
      ▼

```

This sequence should not be interpreted as a rigid universal pipeline.

It represents the current organizational orientation of the repository.

# 20 — Comparative Research

`20-comparative-research/` contains research that compares already externalized Structural Representations.

The primary comparative principle is:

> **Compare Representations, not raw resources.**

This reduces the risk of comparing research objects only through terminology, document structure, or superficial conceptual similarity.

Comparative research may examine relationships such as:

- structural correspondence,
- functional correspondence,
- dependency correspondence,
- hierarchical relationships,
- topological relationships,
- and structural differences.

Comparison does not assume equivalence.

Difference is treated as research evidence.

---

# 30 — Cross-Case Synthesis

`30-cross-case-synthesis/` is reserved for observations that become visible across multiple representation or comparison cases.

Its responsibility is different from individual comparison.

```text
Individual Representation
          ↓
Pairwise / Local Comparison
          ↓
Multiple Comparative Cases
          ↓
Cross-Case Observation
          ↓
Synthesis
```

This domain should therefore be used only when sufficient cross-case evidence exists.

It is not intended to force premature generalization.

---

# Structural Representation as Research Interface

A central hypothesis underlying this repository is that Structural Representation can function as an interface between heterogeneous research objects.

Different domains may use different:

- terminology,
- conceptual vocabularies,
- document structures,
- theoretical assumptions,
- and levels of abstraction.

Direct comparison can therefore become unstable.

Structural Representation provides an intermediate research surface:

```text
Research Object A
       ↓
Structural Representation A
       │
       │
       ▼
Structural Comparison
       ▲
       │
       │
Structural Representation B
       ↑
Research Object B
```

The objective is not to erase domain differences.

The objective is to make structural relationships observable while preserving those differences.

# Research Boundary

This repository distinguishes among four different activities:

```text
Representation
     ≠
Comparison
     ≠
Synthesis
     ≠
Research Output
```

These activities may interact, but they should not be collapsed into one another.

In particular:

- Representation should not be distorted to produce a desired comparison.
- Comparison should not assume structural equivalence.
- Synthesis should not precede sufficient cross-case evidence.
- Research outputs should preserve the provenance of the underlying representations and observations.

The repository therefore preserves both:

- and the developmental process through which Structural Representation research continues to mature.
